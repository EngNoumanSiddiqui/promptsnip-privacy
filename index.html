01Introduction

PromptSnip is a Chrome extension developed by NoumaniaSoft that helps users generate AI-ready prompts from content they intentionally select, enter, or upload. Supported workflows include webpage selections, images, written text, PDFs, audio files, videos, and supported video links.

This policy explains the information PromptSnip may collect and process when providing its features, the service providers involved, storage practices, and how to contact us about privacy requests.

02Information we process

Account information

When you sign in using Google, PromptSnip may receive your name, email address, profile image, Google account identifier, and authentication tokens or session information needed to operate your account. PromptSnip does not receive or store your Google password.

Content you submit

Depending on the feature you choose, this may include:

  • Selected webpage elements, regions, and captured screenshots.
  • Single or multiple uploaded, pasted, or selected images.
  • Text, PDF documents, and additional instructions.
  • Uploaded video and audio files, and supported video URLs.
  • Prompt category selections, generated results, and related preferences.

Local browser information

The extension may store preferences, unfinished drafts, session-related information, and generated prompt history in local extension storage so that work remains available when the popup closes and reopens.

Credits and account usage

To operate its credit system, PromptSnip may process credit balances, daily or monthly usage counts, generation activity, and requests for additional credits.

Technical information

Backend services may process timestamps, error details, API status codes, rate-limit events, and IP addresses where needed for security, service reliability, troubleshooting, or abuse prevention.

Before submitting content: Avoid uploading passwords, financial credentials, confidential business material, or information you do not have permission to share.

03How information is used

PromptSnip uses relevant information to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain account sessions.
  • Generate and regenerate prompts from user-submitted content.
  • Maintain drafts, extension preferences, and available prompt history.
  • Manage generation credits, limits, and credit requests.
  • Prevent abuse, diagnose errors, secure services, and support users.

PromptSnip does not sell personal information or use user data for personalized advertising, lending, or creditworthiness assessments.

04AI processing and service providers

Google Gemini

PromptSnip uses Google Gemini services to generate prompts. Content you submit for a generation request may be transmitted for processing, including text, webpage captures, images, PDFs, audio, video, and supported linked content. Media workflows may use the Gemini Files API for temporary upload and processing, subject to the provider's applicable terms and retention practices.

Supabase

Supabase supports authentication and account-related backend services, such as user records and related operational data.

Cloudflare

Cloudflare Workers provides API infrastructure and may process requests and related technical metadata for service delivery, security, and reliability.

Google sign-in

Google authentication enables account access without requiring users to give their Google password directly to PromptSnip.

05Data sharing and sale

PromptSnip does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers to authenticate users, process user-requested content, generate prompts, manage accounts and credits, protect the service, or comply with legal obligations. Those services operate under their own applicable privacy and security terms.

06Chrome Web Store Limited Use

PromptSnip's use and transfer of information obtained through Chrome extension functionality is intended to support its disclosed prompt-generation features and associated security and account operations. PromptSnip is designed to follow the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

  • Data use is tied to the extension's disclosed functionality.
  • Data is not sold or transferred for personalized advertising.
  • Data is not used for creditworthiness or lending decisions.
  • Human access to personal or sensitive user data must be limited to circumstances allowed under applicable policy, such as explicit consent for support, security, legal compliance, or properly aggregated internal operations.

07Data retention

Local storage

Locally stored drafts, preferences, and history may remain until you delete them using available controls, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall the extension.

Account records

Account and credit-related records may remain while an account is active or as needed for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes.

Media and AI services

Uploaded content may be temporarily handled by external AI services. The provider's retention practices and the backend's configured deletion workflow determine how long temporary copies remain.

Technical logs

Technical records may be retained for monitoring, troubleshooting, security, and abuse prevention based on the services' configurations.

08Data deletion and privacy requests

Available controls in PromptSnip let you manage certain local inputs and history entries. For requests to access, correct, or delete account-related information, write to noumaniasoft@gmail.com, preferably from your registered account email. We may verify ownership before responding.

Deleting local extension history does not necessarily remove account data, technical records, or information held independently by external service providers.

09Data security

PromptSnip uses security controls appropriate to its operations, which may include HTTPS, authentication and session protections, server-side input validation, access controls, and abuse-prevention measures. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10Children's privacy

PromptSnip is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information.

11International processing

PromptSnip uses cloud and AI services that may process information in countries or regions where those providers maintain infrastructure. Applicable legal requirements and provider terms govern such processing.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if features, service providers, data-handling practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current published version. Material changes will be communicated when required by applicable law or platform policies.
