## 1. Introduction

PromptSnip is an AI-powered Chrome extension developed by NoumaniaSoft. It helps users create structured AI prompts from content they intentionally select, enter, or upload.

PromptSnip supports webpage selections, images, text, PDF documents, video files, supported video URLs, and audio files.

This Privacy Policy explains how PromptSnip collects, processes, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the extension.

By using PromptSnip, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### 2.1 Account Information

When you sign in using Google authentication, PromptSnip may receive and process:

- Your name
- Email address
- Google account identifier
- Profile image
- Authentication tokens and session information required to maintain your account

This information is used to authenticate users, manage accounts, provide access to extension features, and associate generation credits with the correct account.

PromptSnip does not receive or store your Google account password.

### 2.2 User-Provided Content

PromptSnip processes content that users intentionally provide through the extension.

This may include:

**Webpage Selection**

- Selected webpage elements
- Selected webpage regions
- Captured screenshots
- Multiple selected regions or elements

**Images**

- Uploaded image files
- Images pasted from the clipboard
- Single-image inputs
- Multiple-image inputs
- Visual references and screenshots

**Text and PDF Documents**

- Text entered or pasted by users
- Uploaded PDF documents
- Additional instructions
- Selected prompt types and preferences

**Video**

- Uploaded video files
- Supported video URLs
- Video content submitted for AI prompt generation

**Audio**

- Uploaded audio files
- Audio content submitted for AI prompt generation

**Generated Prompts**

- AI-generated prompt results
- Prompt-generation preferences
- Prompt titles and relevant metadata
- Prompt history saved through the extension

Submitted content is used to provide the prompt-generation functionality requested by the user.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials, confidential business information, or other sensitive content unless they are authorized to share it and understand that it may be transmitted to external processing services.

### 2.3 Local Browser Storage

PromptSnip uses browser extension storage to maintain relevant information, which may include:

- Extension preferences
- Unfinished inputs or drafts
- Session-related information
- Generated prompt history
- Saved or favorited prompts
- Interface settings

Locally stored information helps preserve the user experience when the extension popup is closed and reopened.

Users can clear inputs and manage stored prompt history through available extension controls.

### 2.4 Credits and Account Usage

PromptSnip provides a credit-based usage system.

To operate this functionality, PromptSnip may process:

- Account identifiers
- Available credit balances
- Daily and monthly usage
- Prompt-generation counts
- Credit adjustment records
- Additional-credit requests
- Request status and related messages

This information is used to manage generation limits, process credit requests, prevent abuse, and maintain service availability.

### 2.5 Technical and Diagnostic Information

PromptSnip's backend services may process limited technical information required to operate and secure the service, including:

- Request timestamps
- API response status
- Error messages
- Usage and rate-limit events
- IP addresses where processed by hosting or security services
- Authentication and service-operation events

This information may be used for security monitoring, abuse prevention, debugging, reliability, and technical support.

PromptSnip does not require access to unrelated browsing history to provide its core functionality.

## 3. How We Use Information

PromptSnip processes information for the following purposes:

1. Authenticating users and maintaining account sessions.
2. Processing content submitted for AI prompt generation.
3. Generating, regenerating, and presenting AI prompts.
4. Maintaining extension settings, drafts, and prompt history.
5. Managing generation credits and usage limits.
6. Processing user-submitted requests for additional credits.
7. Preventing unauthorized use, spam, and abuse.
8. Diagnosing technical issues and maintaining service reliability.
9. Providing technical support and responding to privacy-related requests.
10. Improving the security and functionality of PromptSnip.

PromptSnip does not sell personal information or use personal or sensitive user data for personalized advertising, creditworthiness assessments, or lending purposes.

## 4. AI Processing and External Services

PromptSnip uses third-party infrastructure and AI services to provide its core functionality.

### 4.1 Google Gemini

PromptSnip uses Google's Gemini AI services to process user-submitted content and generate structured AI prompts.

Depending on the feature being used, the submitted information may include:

- Text and additional instructions
- Webpage selections or screenshots
- Images
- PDF documents
- Audio files
- Video files or supported video content

For certain media-processing workflows, PromptSnip may use the Gemini Files API to upload content temporarily for processing.

Content submitted for AI processing is subject to the applicable service provider's data-processing, security, and retention terms.

### 4.2 Supabase

PromptSnip uses Supabase for account authentication and related backend services.

Supabase may process account identifiers, authentication data, profile information, and account-related records necessary for operating the extension.

### 4.3 Cloudflare

PromptSnip uses Cloudflare Workers for backend API processing and associated security and reliability functions.

Cloudflare infrastructure may process API requests, technical metadata, and data transmitted through the backend when users request prompt generation.

### 4.4 Google Authentication

PromptSnip supports Google sign-in.

Google authentication allows users to access their PromptSnip accounts without providing their Google passwords directly to PromptSnip.

Google may process authentication-related information according to its own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Sharing

PromptSnip does not sell or rent personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to:

- Authenticate users
- Generate AI prompts
- Process user-submitted content
- Maintain accounts and credits
- Protect against security threats or abuse
- Operate and maintain the service
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

These services may process data under their respective privacy and security terms.

PromptSnip does not transfer user information to third parties for unrelated marketing or advertising purposes.

## 6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

PromptSnip's use and transfer of information obtained through Chrome extension functionality is intended solely to provide or improve its disclosed prompt-generation features.

PromptSnip adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

- User information is processed for the extension's stated purpose.
- Data transfers are limited to those necessary for the service, security, or legal compliance.
- User data is not sold or transferred for personalized advertising.
- User data is not used for lending or creditworthiness assessments.
- Human access to personal or sensitive user content is restricted to circumstances permitted by the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, such as explicit user consent for support, security requirements, applicable law, or appropriately aggregated and anonymized internal operations.

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Local Storage

Information stored locally by the extension may remain available until users:

- Clear inputs or drafts
- Delete individual history records
- Clear available prompt-history data
- Remove the extension
- Clear the extension's browser storage

### 7.2 Account Data

Account-related information may be retained while a PromptSnip account remains active or for as long as reasonably necessary for security, account administration, service operations, or legal compliance.

### 7.3 Uploaded Content

Uploaded documents, images, audio, and video may be transmitted to external AI services for processing.

Temporary uploaded files may be removed from PromptSnip-controlled processing workflows when no longer needed.

The retention of information held by external service providers is also subject to those providers' applicable data-retention policies.

### 7.4 Technical Logs

Technical records may be retained for operational monitoring, troubleshooting, security, and abuse prevention according to applicable service configurations and retention practices.

## 8. Data Deletion and User Rights

Users can manage some locally stored information directly through the extension, including clearing inputs and deleting prompt-history entries.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of account-related information, users may contact:

**noumaniasoft@gmail.com**

Please use the email address associated with your PromptSnip account when submitting an account deletion request.

We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Deletion requests will be handled according to applicable legal obligations and operational requirements.

Deleting locally stored prompt history does not necessarily remove account-related information or records retained by external service providers.

## 9. Data Security

PromptSnip uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed through the extension and its backend services.

These may include:

- HTTPS connections for supported services
- Authentication and session-management controls
- Server-side validation
- Access controls for backend services
- Rate limiting and abuse prevention
- Error monitoring and security-related logging

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Users are encouraged to protect their Google accounts and avoid submitting information they are not authorized to share.
