# PromptSnip Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 22, 2026**

PromptSnip is a Chrome extension that converts user-selected webpage content, images, screenshots, and written text into AI-ready prompts. This policy explains what information PromptSnip processes and how it is used.

## Information PromptSnip Processes

### Account information

When you sign in with Google, PromptSnip may receive your name, email address, profile image, Google account identifier, and authentication information required to create and maintain your account session.

PromptSnip does not receive or store your Google password.

### User-provided content

PromptSnip processes content that you intentionally provide, including:

* Text entered into the extension
* Images or screenshots uploaded or pasted into the extension
* Webpage elements or regions you explicitly select
* Generated prompts and related preferences

This content is processed only to provide the prompt-generation features requested by you.

### Local browser data

PromptSnip may store your preferences, unfinished inputs, authentication session, and prompt history in your browser’s local extension storage. This allows your work and settings to remain available when the extension popup is closed and reopened.

You can clear inputs and delete stored prompt-history items through the extension.

### Technical information

PromptSnip’s backend services may process limited technical information, such as request timestamps, error information, and IP addresses, for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, and service reliability.

## How Information Is Used

PromptSnip uses information only to:

* Authenticate users
* Generate prompts requested by users
* Save extension preferences and local prompt history
* Prevent abuse and enforce usage limits
* Diagnose errors and maintain service reliability
* Improve the security and operation of PromptSnip

PromptSnip does not use user data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or purposes unrelated to its prompt-generation functionality.

## Service Providers

PromptSnip uses service providers necessary to operate the extension, including:

* Google for user authentication and AI processing
* Supabase for authentication and account services
* Cloudflare for API hosting, security, and request processing

These providers may process information only as necessary to deliver their respective services and according to their own privacy and security terms.

## Data Sharing and Sale

PromptSnip does not sell personal information.

Information may be transferred to the service providers listed above only when necessary to authenticate users, process requested content, generate prompts, secure the service, or comply with legal obligations.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored inputs and prompt history remain in your browser until you clear them, delete individual items, remove the extension, or clear the extension’s storage.

Account information may be retained while your PromptSnip account remains active or as necessary for security, legal, and operational purposes.

To request deletion of account-related information, contact us using the email address below.

## Data Security

PromptSnip uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect processed information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children’s Privacy

PromptSnip is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when PromptSnip’s features, service providers, or legal obligations change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
